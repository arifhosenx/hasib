01 How long does delivery take?+

Orders usually arrive in 1–2 business days in Dhaka and 3–5 business days elsewhere in Bangladesh. Our team will share delivery updates with you.

02 Can I exchange an item?+

Yes. We accept exchanges within 7 days of delivery for sizing issues or manufacturing defects. Items must be unworn and have their original tags.

03 What payment methods do you accept?+

Pay with bKash, Nagad, or cash on delivery in eligible areas. Any advance delivery fee will be confirmed with you when placing your order.
